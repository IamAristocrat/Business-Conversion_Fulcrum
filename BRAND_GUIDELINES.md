# Business Conversion Fulcrum — Brand Guidelines

**Parent brand:** Aristocrat Intelli-Tech (endorsement at every principal identity appearance).

**Business identity:** Business Conversion Fulcrum (BCF) — Your Hyper-Niche Revenue Catalyst Partners.

**Core proposition:** You focus on execution. We find the right buyers. Together, we work to close deals.

**Philosophy:** Survival of the Fittest; define competitive fitness as adaptation to changing customer needs and strong delivery execution.

**Voice:** Precise, commercially literate, restrained and easy to understand. Avoid BDaaS jargon in customer-facing lead copy. No guaranteed outcomes or fabricated client metrics.

**Primary palette:** Obsidian Graphite `#10151B`, Titanium Slate `#303B46`, Rose Copper `#C88F7D`, Signal Teal `#27C7B8`, Porcelain `#F4F2EE`, Deep Steel `#18252E`. Reserve teal primarily for meaningful calls to action and status indicators.

**Logo:** The `assets/logo-mark.svg` is a placeholder stylized mark; replace only with an authorised Aristocrat Intelli-Tech master asset before brand sign-off. Do not claim trademark or registered-mark status without confirmation.

**Visuals:** Technical/architectural composition; precision fulcrum as focal motif. No generic robot, lions, stock handshake collages, imaginary partners or invented testimonials.

**Public exposure:** No public display of client identity or case study without specific written consent. Present company as privately operated, but do not claim verified military or defence certification.

**Release:** Pure public-facing static pages; no stored submissions, CRM, hidden analytics or server side collection. On qualification, user reviews the summary and may voluntarily open WhatsApp or call `+91 98945 95035`.
